Hello everyone

We are very proud to announce that the website [GamingOnLinux](http://www.gamingonlinux.com) has revealed OpenMW as the winner of their [Game of the Year awards](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/goty.php?module=category&category_id=7) in the *Favourite FOSS game engine reimplementation* category.

Competing against other respected and important projects like [ScummVM](https://www.scummvm.org/) and [OpenRA](https://www.openra.net/), this means a lot, and we are very honoured. A BIG thank you to everyone who voted for OpenMW!

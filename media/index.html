Release commentaries and videos

Our official YouTube channel, named MrOpenMW, is where the release commentaries are uploaded. We try to make two videos (one for OpenMW and one for OpenMW-CS) for every release, covering the most important changes to the engine. The commentaries are currently made by the brilliant Atahualpa, and have been for the last few years. Most videos up to version 0.35 were made by the legendary WeirdSexy. Filling in for Atahualpa for the 0.46.0 release, the terrific johnnyhostile has now joined the team to collaborate with Atahualpa in future OpenMW videos.

Christmas is over and Santa has left a little present for everyone.

On the 25th of December a [patch for TES3MP](http://steamcommunity.com/groups/mwmulti#announcements/detail/1600332806171724427) landed, fixing bugs, enhancing server scripting and introducing a new interesting feature. Version 0.6.2 allows players to play NPC sounds and animations on themselves. Essentially, your character can now say almost anything that an NPC of your race and gender can say, using the new /speech command, and you can also play any of the NPC idle animations using /anim.

[![](https://openmw.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/New-Bitmap-Image-1-300x36.jpg)](https://openmw.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/New-Bitmap-Image-1.bmp)

In addition to that, TES3MP has upgraded to the latest version of OpenMW, meaning that new features from OpenMW’s main branch such as Scrawl’s keyboard shortcuts are now included.

Happy holidays.

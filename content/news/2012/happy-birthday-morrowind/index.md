Morrowind is 10 years old today! What are you going to do to celebrate this?

You can:

- Try [Morrowind Overhaul](http://morrowindoverhaul.rpgitalia.net/), which is a stunning compilation of many graphical mods. Your Morrowind will never had looked so shiny and actual. Check the [screenshots](http://morrowindoverhaul.rpgitalia.net/screenshots?show=gallery) if you don’t believe me!
- Try [Tamriel Rebuilt](http://tamriel-rebuilt.org), which is older than MOrrowind itself, and that aims to offer the **whole** Morrowind continent (and not only the central island).
- Try our lastest release of OpenMW: the [0.14.0](/news/2012/openmw-0-14-0-released) one, which brings up many exciting features.
- Listen to the [amazing](http://kotaku.com/5905585/morrowind-really-did-have-the-best-elder-scrolls-music-didnt-it) original soundtrack
- [Educate](http://www.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Morrowind) yourself about Morrowind’s background

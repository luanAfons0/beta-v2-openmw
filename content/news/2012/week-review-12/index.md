Hello everyone !

The major news for the week comes via **Mark** (creator of the [Crystal Scrolls](http://crystalscrolls.sourceforge.net/ "The crystal scrolls")): he [nearly finished](http://openmw.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=577#p7049) multiple esp/esm support! There are numerous problems, but things are progressing: you’ll soon be able to travel to Solstheim!

**garvek**, our new developer who is also part of the [dugeonhack](http://dungeonhack.sourceforge.net/Main_Page) project, started working on cmake fixes and making OpenMW compile with MSVC. The DungeonHack team is deciding whether to help OpenMW reach basic functionality and then base DungeonHack off our engine. Sounds good to me!

**zini** returned to working on the script implementation. Excellent!

**jhooks1** is continuing work on the physics system. Nothing really spectacular and the ogre3d enabled new animations system still is not able to handle robes and some creatures. If someone is an expert in animations then he is welcome on our forums.

[Gentoo](http://www.gentoo.org/) overlay for OpenMW has reached alpha status and awaits testing. Big thanks (if it’s working correctly) to **edmondo**.

Lurkers of the irc channel are reporting that **scrawl** said:

> btw I have started with rewriting shader system to allow [GLSL](http://www.opengl.org/documentation/glsl/ "glsl"). I am a making sort of a library out of it, it will be similar to ogre [RTShaderSystem](http://www.ogre3d.org/forums/viewtopic.php?f=4&t=52219) but much better. then user can put in a settings file which shader language he wants :P

Ho, and it seems like **pvdk** and **k1ll** are active again :)
